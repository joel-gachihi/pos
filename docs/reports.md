# Reports

## Daily Sales Report

Filters:
- business date
- branch
- cashier
- payment method
- customer

Columns/metrics:
- invoice number
- time
- customer
- subtotal
- discount
- tax
- total
- amount paid
- balance
- status
- cashier/user

Totals:
- gross sales
- discounts
- taxes
- net sales
- payments
- refunds
- outstanding balance

## Daily Transaction Report

Include all relevant posted financial events, not only sales: sales, payments, expenses, refunds, transfers, adjustments, and reversals.

## Account Report

Show account code/name, opening balance, debits, credits, movement, and closing balance for a selected period.

## Individual Account Report

A detailed ledger for one account, including date, journal number, source document, description, debit, credit, and running balance.

## Trial Balance

For selected date/period, list every account with debit and credit balances. Total debits and credits must agree.

## Income Statement

At minimum:

```text
Revenue
- Cost of Sales
= Gross Profit
- Operating Expenses
+/- Other Income/Expenses
= Profit/(Loss)
```

## Statement of Financial Position

```text
Assets
Liabilities
Equity
```

Verify that assets equal liabilities plus equity.

## Statement of Changes in Equity

Show opening equity, capital contributions, profit/loss, drawings/dividends, other equity movements, and closing equity.

## Cash Flow Statement

Classify cash movements into operating, investing, and financing activities. Provide direct/indirect presentation according to the organization's reporting policy.

## Daily Profit/Loss

A management view for a selected business date, derived from posted revenue and expense accounts. If inventory/cost-of-sales data is incomplete, clearly label gross-profit metrics as incomplete rather than inventing values.

## Report Security

Reports must obey organization/branch/user permissions and should record report access where required by policy.
